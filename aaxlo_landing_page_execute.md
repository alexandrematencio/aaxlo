# AAXLO — Landing Page

## Architecture Notes

- Page arc: Focus → Meaning → Action
- Every CTA uses different language and emotional angle
- Interactive micro-moments between sections build engagement before any real ask
- No two sections feel the same — vary rhythm, length, interaction type

---

## SECTION 5 — What We Do

**Headline:**

We handle the part you keep putting off.

**Five blocks:**

**Visibility**
Your Google Business Profile, local search rankings, directory listings. Set up right the first time, or fixed if they've been sitting half-done. When someone searches for what you do nearby, you show up.

**Web**
A website that does its job. Fast, clean, built for phones first. The kind of site where someone lands and calls you, books you, or buys — instead of bouncing.

**Content**
Social media that stays active without draining your evenings. Posts that sound like your business, not like a template. A pipeline that keeps going without you babysitting it.

**Automation**
AI that takes over the repetitive work. Chatbots that answer common questions. Review responses that go out on their own. WhatsApp messages handling bookings while you're with a customer. Things that used to be reserved for companies ten times your size.

**Custom builds**
For when you need something that doesn't exist yet. A workflow built around how your business actually operates. An app. A tool. Something your competitors don't have.

**No CTA in this section.** Let the information build trust without asking for anything.
