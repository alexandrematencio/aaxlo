## Core Brand Identity & Aesthetics

### Overall Style

> **Digital Neo-Brutalism meets Swiss Typographic Style.**
> The feel is akin to a high-tech architectural blueprint or a live wireframe brought to life.

**Vibe:** Authoritative, transparent, highly engineered, and premium. It communicates *"we build advanced systems"* without relying on cliché tech imagery (like glowing brains or abstract nodes).

---

### Typography — The Hero Element

Typography acts as both the messaging and the primary graphical element.

**Font Family**
**SWITZER** — A stark, ultra-clean, geometric sans-serif (cf. Helvetica Now, Inter, or a custom grotesque).

**Extreme Hierarchy**
- **Hero Statements:** Massive, screen-filling text spanning multiple lines (e.g., *"Human Expertise, expanded by AI"*)
- **Structural Labels:** Tiny, widely tracked (spaced), all-caps text for utility, navigation, and micro-copy (e.g., `SERVICES`, `WORK`)

**Data-Driven Styling**
Continued use of slashed zeros (`0̸`) and monospaced-style alignments for numbers and data points to reinforce the "engineered" DNA of the brand.

---

### Web Layout & UI Elements — The Grid

The strict grid translates directly into the web UI, creating a transparent, wireframe-like structure.

**Visible Thin Borders (0.5–1px Lines)**
The defining characteristic. Sections, service cards, and grid columns are separated by thin, solid lines — white on black backgrounds, black on light backgrounds. Everything lives in a clearly defined box.

**Modular Bento Box Layouts**
Content is organized into distinct rectangular compartments. Services (Business, Design, Marketing, Development) are displayed in a rigid 4-column or 2×2 grid setup.

**Process Timelines**
Workflows are visualized using stark, linear steps (e.g., *"Step 1: Human Insight," "Step 2: AI Synthesis"*) connected by thin lines or geometric nodes, resembling technical flowcharts.

**Massive Structural Footers**
The footer is treated as a major design element — giant grid layouts organize company links, resources, and contact info, often anchored by a massive logo.

---

### The Dual-Tone Color Palette

**Primary Backgrounds**
A stark interplay between pure/deep **Black** and warm **Off-White / Light Beige**. Sections alternate between these two extremes to create rhythm on the page.

**Typography Colors**
High contrast only.
- Pure White or Beige text on Black backgrounds
- Pure Black text on Beige/White backgrounds

**The Singular Accent**
The muted, salmon-pink/coral circle (with diagonal line hatching) used in the `AAXLO` logo remains the **only pop of color** across the entire digital and print ecosystem.

---

### Absence of Standard Media

| ❌ Avoid | ✅ Use Instead |
|---|---|
| Stock photography | Scale of typography |
| Soft drop shadows | Mathematical precision of grid lines |
| Glowing gradients | Stark black/beige contrast |
| 3D renders | Functional, structural composition |

## Logo

### Construction

#### Logomark

The core symbol is a geometric abstract representation of the brand name's initials, subtly integrated into a single, cohesive mark.

**The Foundation — AA**
The base features two interlocking or fused uppercase `A`s rendered with strong, clean, upward-pointing diagonal lines — representing stability, growth, and the foundational partnership of Alex and Aymeric.

**The Core — X (eXtended)**
Intersecting dynamically within the upper structure of the fused `A`s is a stylized `X`. Prominent and technological, it may be formed by two intersecting data streams or subtle nodes at the intersection point. It symbolizes the *"eXtended"* intelligence and core AI power of the agency.

#### Full Logo Wordmark

- **Font:** Switzer [Semibold]
- **Usage:** Used as-is, no modification

#### Glyph Construction

- **Form:** Disk / 21°
- **Rotation:** 45° anticlockwise

---

### Logo Clear Space

Clear space (or "safe space") is the mandatory, invisible buffer zone surrounding the logo that must remain free of text, images, or edge constraints. It ensures legibility, professionalism, and visual balance across all materials.

> **For AAXLO, the glyphed `O` defines the clear space unit.**

## Components

### Frames as Boxes

**Style References:** Neo-Brutalist · Swiss Style (International Typographic Style) · Minimalist Editorial · Corporate Blueprint

**Vibe:** Clinical, authoritative, modern, structural, and highly organized. Mimics the look of a technical audit, wireframe, or architectural blueprint — with refined typographic sensibilities.

---

### Typography — The Primary Visual Driver

Geometric or neo-grotesque sans-serif (cf. Helvetica Now, Inter, Roboto).

**Hierarchy & Contrast**

| Level | Style | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Display Text | Massive, heavy-weight | `01` |
| Secondary Headings | Large, light or regular weight | *"Digital Presence"* |
| Metadata & Labels | Tiny, tracked-out, all-caps | `CATEGORY` · `SCORE` · `IDENTITY` |

**Stylistic Quirks**
Use of the **slashed zero** (`0̸`) in large numerals (e.g., `01`) adds a technical, coding, or mathematical flavor.
